01 · About

Research through building.

I am a graduate researcher in Computer Science interested in how emerging technologies can support training, rehabilitation, safety, and everyday decision-making.

My work spans the full prototyping process: defining a research problem, designing interaction flows, implementing hardware–software systems, integrating real-time data, and evaluating the resulting user experience. I am particularly interested in systems that move beyond a screen and respond to users through physical interaction, immersive environments, and context-aware feedback.

01

Human-centered

Technology should be grounded in real user needs, not added for novelty.

02

System-oriented

I examine the complete interaction loop across sensing, inference, feedback, and behavior.

03

Evidence-aware

I separate demonstrated results, prototypes, and ongoing research claims.

03 · Selected Projects

Research prototypes and systems.

Selected work across immersive interaction, multimodal feedback, and human-centered AI.

01
VR · AI · Multimodal FeedbackOngoing

ARTEMIS — AI-Powered VR Archery Training

A VR archery training system that connects physical bow interaction, motion events, physiological signals, and AI-generated feedback. The system provides shot-level guidance and session-level reports while maintaining a real-time training flow.

ContributionUnity VR implementation, interaction logic, system integration, feedback UI, and evaluation planning.
  • Unity
  • Meta Quest 3
  • FastAPI
  • Arduino
  • WebSocket
02
HCI · In-Vehicle AIResearch

In-Car Child Intervention AI Agent

An HCI research project examining when an in-vehicle AI agent should intervene in rear-seat child situations and when direct parental involvement remains necessary.

FocusIntervention episode taxonomy, level definitions, naturalistic driving context, and evaluation measures.
  • NDS Analysis
  • Codebook Design
  • Human Factors
03
VR · Rehabilitation · BiosignalPrototype

EMG-Based VR Vocational Training

A rehabilitation-oriented VR training prototype that uses surface EMG signals to connect physical exertion with task performance in an immersive car-wash scenario.

FocusEMG threshold interaction, VR task design, real-time feedback, and accessibility-oriented training.
  • Unity
  • sEMG
  • Arduino
  • Signal Processing
04
Mobile · Speech AI · Social CarePrototype

AI-Assisted Welfare Documentation

A mobile workflow that converts spoken field notes into structured summaries and document-ready records to reduce repetitive administrative work.

FocusSpeech-to-text, LLM summarization, mobile interface, and structured document generation.
  • Flutter
  • Whisper
  • LLM
  • Document Automation
